# Acceptable Use Policy (Sample)
**Version:** 0.1 • **Date:** 2025-09-28
**Owner:** CISO • **Applies to:** All employees and contractors

## Purpose
Define acceptable use of information assets.

## Policy
- Use organization devices for business purposes.
- Do not share credentials; MFA required where supported.
- Report security incidents within 24 hours.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## References
- NIST CSF PR.AC-1, PR.AT-1
